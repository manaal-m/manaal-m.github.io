I am a first-year MSc student in Health Technologies & Data Science at JSSAHER, with a BSc in Medical Imaging Technology which gave me direct clinical experience with MRI modalities and patient care. For my MSc thesis, I am working on developing a DL pipeline for early Parkinson's disease detection using raw DWI signals instead of usual DTI metrics and benchmarking them to see what information is lost when we summarise an entire DWI signal into summaries like FA or MD. I have a first-author publication in IEEE Access on interpretable ML for maternal health risk stratification, with active submissions in cardiac MRI segmentation and cross-subject EEG seizure detection.
